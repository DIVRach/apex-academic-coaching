Frequently Asked Questions

How do you match students with the right tutor?

We look at your child's academic goals, learning style, and personality to pair them with a tutor who will inspire and challenge them.

What happens if a student needs to reschedule a session?

We offer a flexible 24-hour cancellation policy. Sessions rescheduled outside of that window can easily be moved to another day that week.

Do you offer in-person or online sessions?

We provide highly interactive online tutoring via a digital whiteboard, as well as in-person sessions depending on local tutor availability.
